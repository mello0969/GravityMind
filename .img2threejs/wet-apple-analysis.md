# Wet apple reference analysis

## Identification

- Single whole red apple, frontal three-quarter-neutral view, isolated against black.
- Near-radial organic body with a deep top cavity and one narrow woody stem.
- The single view hides the rear surface; rear color and droplet continuity must be inferred.

## Macro form

- Height and width are nearly equal, with broad rounded shoulders and a slightly narrowed base.
- The upper contour dips around the stem socket; the bottom resolves into shallow rounded lobes.
- The silhouette is smooth and continuous. Faceted or pumpkin-like segmentation would be a mismatch.

## Meso form

- A dark recessed cavity surrounds the stem root.
- The stem is mostly upright with a slight irregular bend and a darker cut tip.
- Broad, low-amplitude longitudinal lobes are visible through highlight and contour variation.

## Surface and material

- Skin albedo ranges from deep burgundy in the left and lower shadow to saturated scarlet on the upper-right face.
- Gloss is layered: a moderately rough waxy skin under sharper, irregular water highlights.
- Dense clear droplets vary from pinpoints to larger elongated beads, with bright rims and darker refracted centers.
- Tiny pale lenticels remain visible between droplets and must not form a regular grid.
- The stem is dry, rough, fibrous brown and visually distinct from the wet skin.

## Lighting evidence

- Large soft frontal-left source shapes the main skin highlight without flattening the apple.
- A tighter upper-right response reveals droplets and brighter red skin.
- Edges and lower body remain substantially darker, preserving volume against black.

## Fidelity priorities

1. Smooth near-square silhouette and recessed stem socket.
2. Natural red value variation without baked white highlight bands.
3. Separate waxy-skin and clear-droplet roughness responses.
4. Irregular multi-scale droplet distribution, densest over the upper half.
5. Dry embedded stem with no visible attachment gap.

## Runtime route

The hero rotates through all azimuths, so a single front projection would reveal a seam and baked lighting on the rear. Use reference-derived procedural color/roughness evidence on cylindrical UVs plus instanced droplet geometry. The unseen rear is an explicitly inferred continuation of the observed material.
